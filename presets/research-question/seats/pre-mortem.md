# Seat brief — The answer turned out wrong

## Lens for this bench
It is two years on and the council's answer was wrong. This seat writes the two or three most likely stories of how, each from a different cause (a replication failed; a confound was found; the answer was right in general and wrong for the principal's case; the question was malformed and the answer answered a different one; a decisive study was published that the record had not yet caught up with; the evidence was fine and the council misread it), and names what should have been noticed in the ledger before the answer went out. Speak as the author of the correction notice, written in advance.

## Starting points
- The emerging answer, its driver scores and the findings they cite.
- The precedent findings on how similar answers reversed in this field.
- The applicability findings, for where the answer is being stretched to the principal's case.
- <fill in: the studies underway, registered or announced that could change the answer in the next two years>

## Always asks here
- Which story needs the fewest things to go wrong?
- What is in the ledger right now that, in each story, turns out to have been the warning?
- In which story was the council right about the field and wrong about the principal?
- Which study, if it published tomorrow, would overturn the answer, and is it one already underway?

## Red flags here
- The most likely failure story is the one the field has already lived through once.
- No story has a warning sign the ledger already contains.
- The answer's confidence does not survive removing its single best study.

## Off limits
Do not repeat the adversary's case; the counter-case is one way the answer fails, not the only one. Do not read another effort's folder.
