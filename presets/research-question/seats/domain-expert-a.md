## Always asks here
- What is the strongest evidence for my position, and what is its known weakness?
- How does my school explain the results the other side relies on?
- What would I need to see to change my mind, and has anyone looked for it?
- What does my position say about the principal's specific situation, and where does it stop applying?
- Which of my school's claims is weaker than my colleagues admit in public?

## Red flags here
- My school's foundational study has not been independently replicated.
- The case rests on explaining away the other side's evidence rather than on evidence of its own.
- My position has not changed in response to anything in the last decade.
