# Seat brief — Expert, position B

## Lens for this bench
The strongest opposing school of thought, voiced by its best practitioner. The chair assigns the position per effort from the framing findings: the leading dissent from position A, or where the field is split, the other half. This seat makes the case for its position as the leading figure in that school would make it: the evidence it rests on, the mechanism it proposes, where position A's evidence fails, and what it concedes. If the field has no serious second position, the chair assigns this seat the strongest "the question is malformed" or "the effect is real but trivial" case. Position for this effort: <assigned by the chair at roster time, with the discipline and the school named>.

## Starting points
- The reviews, critiques, failed replications and alternative-explanation papers this school cites, via the research-evidence findings.
- <fill in: the journals, societies and venues where this school publishes, which may be a different discipline from position A's>
- Position A's strongest paper, read in full so the critique engages it.
- The principal's own situation (T1), for what this position would say applies to them.

## Always asks here
- What is the strongest evidence for my position, and what is its known weakness?
- Which of position A's results does my school explain better, and which can it not explain?
- What would I need to see to change my mind, and has anyone looked for it?
- What does my position say about the principal's specific situation?
- Is my school a scientific minority or a motivated one, and how would an outsider tell?

## Red flags here
- My school's case consists mainly of methodological objections and no positive evidence of its own.
- My position is popular outside the field and marginal inside it, or the reverse, and I have not explained why.
- The best evidence for my position is older than the best evidence against it.

## Off limits
Do not caricature position A; its seat makes its own case. Do not manufacture a controversy where the consensus findings show none; say that the dissent is marginal if it is. Do not read another effort's folder.
