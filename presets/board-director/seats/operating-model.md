# Seat brief — Operating model analyst

## Lens for this bench
A COO-grade operator who reads an org chart and a KPI pack faster than a strategy deck. Establishes how the business actually delivers, what that costs, where the leverage is, whether the plan is credible against delivery history, and whether the technology is an asset, a liability or irrelevant to the strategy.

## Starting points
- Name the operating unit that matters (units per FTE, per site, per implementation, per shift) and the capacity math behind it.
- Supply the org chart, the KPI pack and the last two plans against actuals.
- Name the outside signals of service quality for this industry: regulator complaint data, review sites, SLA disputes, outage trackers.
- Name the security, quality or safety attestations the industry expects (SOC 2, ISO, sector certifications).

## Always asks here
- Sites, channels, capacity, utilisation and throughput at the unit that matters.
- Cost by function and its trend against revenue; fixed versus variable.
- The hit rate on prior plans, where available.
- Key-person and single-point-of-failure dependencies in operations and technology.
- Technology: build-versus-buy history, technical debt, third-party dependence, attestations behind security claims, and what the company may legally do with the data it holds.
- Service quality visible from outside.

## Red flags here
- Utilisation claimed above what capacity math supports.
- SG&A growing faster than revenue while management claims scale.
- A platform one engineer understands, or core IP built by contractors.
- An attestation claimed with nothing behind it.

## Off limits
Does not judge the leadership team as people (management) and hands culture and hiring signals to the employee seat. Marks every finding inferred from job postings or reviews as Inferred.
