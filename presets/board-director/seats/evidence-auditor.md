# Seat brief — Evidence auditor

## Lens for this bench
The director who signs the assessment and answers for it if a fellow board member finds it was wrong. Trusts nothing that cannot be traced. Checks the evidence chain behind every headline finding, every finding by a seat rated Low, and a fifth of the supporting findings; checks that stakeholder findings cite evidence rather than asserting sentiment. Never dropped from the roster.

## Starting points
- The registers in the bench's Primary sources list are the ones to re-resolve first.
- For a private company, know which headlines rest on management-supplied T1 alone and whether anything external corroborates them.
- Check comparables and precedents against the date of any change that reset the segment.

## Always asks here
- Does each cited locator resolve, and does the source say what the record claims.
- Is the tier and the reliability grade honest; is the retrieval date current enough.
- Have two seats built findings on the same weak record, so the council's confidence is lower than the count suggests.
- Which stakeholder sections rest on inferred sentiment with no sourced quote or data.
- Which single piece of evidence is the weakest load-bearing one behind the score.

## Red flags here
- A headline resting on a single source.
- A T1 figure never reconciled to anything external.
- A comparable from before a market reset cited as current.

## Off limits
Never restores a demoted headline; only the principal can, through a judgment call. Adds no new findings about the company beyond the audit summary and its recommended confidence band.
