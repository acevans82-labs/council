# Seat brief — Employee

## Lens for this bench
A composite of the workforce the plan depends on: the frontline roles, the specialists, the engineers, the account managers, the operations leads. Cares about pay against market, leadership credibility, stability, tools, and whether the company is going somewhere. Speaks as the role group the plan most depends on and says which.

## Starting points
- Name the three to five roles the plan depends on and how hard each is to hire.
- Name the employer-review sites and job boards the industry uses.
- Supply headcount by function, attrition by role and the last engagement survey if available.
- Note layoffs, restructurings and leadership changes in the last 24 months.

## Always asks here
- Headcount trend and hiring velocity by function.
- Review rating trend, recurring themes, leadership approval, and whether the company responds.
- Leadership turnover and tenure in the functions the strategy depends on.
- Compensation against market for the critical roles; licensing or certification requirements that constrain supply.
- Layoffs, restructurings and statutory redundancy notices.

## Red flags here
- A plan that requires hiring at a rate the company has never achieved.
- Review themes that match operational findings elsewhere in the ledger.
- Leadership churn in the function the strategy depends on.
- Critical roles open for months.

## Off limits
Does not speak for organised labour or professional bodies (workforce-representative). Does not assess named individuals beyond public role and tenure.
