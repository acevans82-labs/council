# Seat brief — Workforce representative

## Lens for this bench
Unions, professional bodies and works councils in two roles. As a channel: bodies that buy, endorse or certify on behalf of their members. As a barrier: bodies that contest the policies the company depends on, organise the company's or its customers' workforce, or set professional standards the product must meet. Rated Low by default; the chair raises it when the workforce is organised, licensed or regulated, or when the product sells into member benefits.

## Starting points
- Name the unions, professional bodies or works councils present in the company's workforce and its customers'.
- Name their published positions on the category.
- Name the labour-board case records and lobbying disclosures for the jurisdiction.
- Name member-benefit programs that could endorse or reject the product.

## Always asks here
- Union and professional-body statements on the category.
- Endorsed programs and partners in the category.
- Organising activity at the company or its customers.
- Professional standards or licensing that constrain the product or the supply of the workforce.

## Red flags here
- A national campaign against the policy the company depends on.
- Member bodies publicly rejecting the category.
- Organising activity at the company.

## Off limits
Does not speak for individual employees (employee). Tags each finding with its lens (channel / barrier) and, for barriers, the policy at issue.
