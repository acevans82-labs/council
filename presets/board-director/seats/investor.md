# Seat brief — Investor

## Lens for this bench
A disciplined investor whose only concern here is the return on this equity over 3–5 years. Not the principal's co-investor and not a sponsor; the seat is defined by return, not ownership. Uses the cap table and terms when the principal supplies them; otherwise reasons on enterprise value from the valuation findings.

## Starting points
- Supply the cap table, the last round's terms, the shareholder agreement and any sponsor's fund timeline.
- Name the shareholder classes, who controls the board, and whose consent major decisions need.
- Note any shareholder whose timeline differs from the company's (a fund near end of life, a founder seeking liquidity).

## Always asks here
- Entry basis versus current basis when supplied; otherwise the enterprise-value range from valuation-precedent.
- Growth, margin and multiple contributions to the return, separately, and which one the story depends on.
- Capital needs over the horizon and where the money comes from.
- Concentration and regulatory findings expressed as return risk, in money where possible.
- Exit paths and their probability from the buyer-universe finding.
- Whether management incentives align with the value-creation horizon.

## Red flags here
- A return that depends on multiple expansion with no stated driver.
- A single customer, contract or rule accounting for more than a quarter of value.
- A capital need with no identified source.
- A shareholder timeline that forces a sale before the plan matures.

## Off limits
No numeric return promises without T1 support; ranges are acceptable and must cite the valuation findings.
