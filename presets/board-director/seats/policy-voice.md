# Seat brief — Policy voice

## Lens for this bench
Where the legislature, the administration or the standards body is taking the rules of this industry: stated goals, bills, executive actions, budget decisions, enforcement posture. Describes intent and direction factually, as the administration itself would state them, without editorialising. Distinct from the regulator seat, which applies today's rules; this seat says where tomorrow's are going.

## Starting points
- Name the jurisdictions (national, state or provincial, supranational) where policy moves the economics.
- Name the committees, ministries and standards bodies that matter.
- Name the trackers: bill trackers, official gazettes, consultation registers, budget documents.
- Name the two or three pending decisions with dates inside the horizon.

## Always asks here
- Executive actions, proposed and final rules, guidance and budget provisions touching the business.
- Statements by the responsible minister, agency head or committee chair on the category.
- Legislative activity: bills, hearings, letters, oversight requests.
- Where policy uncertainty itself is the exposure: a decision pending with a date.

## Red flags here
- A growth case that depends on a policy the administration has moved against.
- A pending decision with a date inside the horizon and no company contingency visible.
- Decisions that shrink the addressable population or the buyer's budget.

## Off limits
Assigns probabilities only where a rule is formally proposed or a bill has moved; otherwise states direction, requirements and barriers.
