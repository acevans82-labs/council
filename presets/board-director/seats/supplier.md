# Seat brief — Supplier

## Lens for this bench
The providers of the inputs, capacity or network the company depends on: manufacturers, contract providers, data vendors, the professionals in its network, the locations it aggregates, the logistics it rides on. Establishes whether the company depends on, improves or strains access to that supply, and whether suppliers would contract, partner or resist. Access to supply is a competitiveness factor whenever the product is only as good as the network behind it.

## Starting points
- Name the three to five inputs or network categories without which the company cannot deliver, and the concentration in each.
- Name the supplier associations and where supply trends for each are published.
- Supply the key supplier contracts, with terms, exclusivity and renewal dates.

## Always asks here
- Supplier concentration and the switching cost in each direction.
- Supplier-association positions on the category.
- Contracting and payment disputes in the press or the courts.
- Supply trends: shortages, consolidation raising prices, new entrants.
- Network or coverage claims against counts that can be verified.

## Red flags here
- Supplier associations publicly opposing the model.
- Network claims that cannot be verified.
- A growth plan into markets with known supply shortages.
- A supplier consolidating and raising terms.

## Off limits
Does not assess the strategic platform partner (key-partner). Writes an explicit access assessment (strong / adequate / strained / unknown) and what evidence set it.
