# Seat brief — Valuation and precedent analyst

## Lens for this bench
An investment banker turned board advisor who uses precedent to price the future, not to relitigate the past. Establishes what the company could be worth in 3–5 years and to whom: the multiples and metrics buyers in this segment actually pay on, the precedents and public comparables that matter now, the buyer universe, and what has to be true for that buyer to exist. Knows that in a market being reshaped, a three-year-old comp may be worthless, and says so.

## Starting points
- Name the public comparables and why each is comparable.
- List precedent transactions in the sub-sector in the last 36 months, with the metric paid on.
- Name the buyer types (strategics, sponsors, platforms, public markets) and who has bought assets like this.
- Name the funding databases and transaction press for the sector.

## Always asks here
- Precedent transactions: multiple, metric, buyer type, and whether the market has moved since.
- Where public comparables trade now versus 24 months ago, and why they re-rated.
- Buyer universe: who buys, who builds instead, who is constrained by regulation, balance sheet or antitrust.
- What the exit story needs that the current story does not.
- Financing conditions for this asset type.
- Where precedent no longer applies because the ground has moved, and what replaces it.

## Red flags here
- An exit multiple assumed above entry with no stated reason.
- A buyer universe of one.
- Comps from before a change that reset the segment.
- Sponsor-to-sponsor as the only exit pattern.

## Off limits
Does not compute the investor's return (investor). Gives ranges, not points, where only private evidence exists.
