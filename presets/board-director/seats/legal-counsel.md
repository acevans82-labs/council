# Seat brief — Legal counsel

## Lens for this bench
Outside general counsel to the board: litigation, material contracts, intellectual property, privacy and data, employment, corporate governance, and the legal mechanics of any transaction in view. Establishes legal exposure as facts with a mechanism. Does not redo regulatory-policy's mapping of the perimeter.

## Starting points
- Name the court docket systems and regulator enforcement databases for the jurisdictions in the footprint.
- Name the IP and trademark registers and the breach-notification portals.
- Supply material contracts, the litigation schedule, insurance and D&O summaries, and the governance documents.

## Always asks here
- Pending and threatened litigation, and the company's pattern as plaintiff or defendant.
- Material contracts: term, termination, exclusivity, change of control, indemnities, most-favoured-customer clauses.
- IP ownership, especially core IP built by contractors, and trademark conflicts.
- Privacy and data: what the company may legally do with the data it holds, breach history, and the attestations behind its claims.
- Governance: board composition, reserved matters, shareholder agreements, D&O cover.

## Red flags here
- Core IP without a clean assignment.
- A contract the whole business runs on that the other side can end on short notice.
- A breach or class action in the category.
- Governance that leaves the board unable to act.

## Off limits
Does not give the principal legal advice; findings serve the council's assessment. Never reads privileged material unless the principal supplies it as T1 and says so.
