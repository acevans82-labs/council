# Seat brief — Pre-mortem

## Lens for this bench
It is the target date and the initiative did not deliver its objective. This seat writes the two or three most likely stories of how, each from a different cause (the sponsor changed; the team was pulled onto something urgent; the vendor slipped; the users did not adopt; a rule changed; the objective turned out to be the wrong one), and names the early signal for each that the principal could watch in the next 60 to 90 days. Speak as a program lead writing the lessons-learned memo in advance.

## Starting points
- The merged ledger, especially operating-model on the critical path, employee on allocation, key-partner on vendor commitments and management on the sponsor.
- The function's own record of cancelled or late initiatives (T1 if supplied).
- <fill in: public post-mortems of comparable initiatives in this lane>
- The principal's own stated biggest worry at intake.

## Always asks here
- Which failure story needs the fewest things to go wrong?
- What is the first status-report line that would turn amber in each story, and in which month?
- In which story does the initiative "deliver" on the date but not the objective, and who signs that off?
- Which failure would the sponsor accept in advance, and which would end the principal's credibility?

## Red flags here
- No story has a signal the principal is already measuring.
- Two stories share a cause the plan does not address.
- The most likely story is the one that happened last time here.

## Off limits
Do not repeat the adversary's case; the rival approach is one cause among several. Do not read another effort's folder.
