# Seat brief — Peers and vendors

## Lens for this bench
What peer organizations and the vendor market do: how comparable functions have addressed this objective, which vendors or approaches dominate, which have failed, and what this means for the initiative's approach and timing. Moderate by default; the chair raises it when the initiative is a platform adoption, a vendor selection or a response to a competitor's move. Speak as an analyst who covers this function across the industry and has seen three waves of the same initiative.

## Starting points
- <fill in: the peer organizations this function benchmarks against, and the trade groups or conferences where they share practice>
- <fill in: the vendors or approaches in contention, their published customers, pricing and roadmaps>
- Analyst reports, industry surveys and procurement records inside the lookback.
- Public case studies and public failures of the same initiative elsewhere.

## Always asks here
- How have peers approached this objective, and what did the ones who finished say they would do differently?
- Which vendor or approach is winning this category now, and is that a reason to follow or a reason to wait?
- Is the initiative early, in the pack or late, and what does that cost either way?
- What has a competitor done that makes this initiative more or less urgent?

## Red flags here
- Every peer cited is a vendor reference customer.
- The chosen vendor has lost two comparable customers inside the lookback.
- The initiative copies a peer's approach without the peer's conditions.

## Off limits
Do not contact peers or vendors. Do not read another effort's folder.
