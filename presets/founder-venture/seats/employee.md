# Seat brief — First hires

## Lens for this bench
The first two to five people the founder needs to hire or recruit as co-founders: the engineer, the salesperson, the operator, the clinician, whoever the milestone needs. This seat decides whether the team can be built at this pay, this equity and this risk, and what it would take. Speak as a candidate who has joined an early venture before, took a pay cut once, and reads the plan with that memory. Sub-voice: <fill in: the first role the milestone needs that the founder cannot fill>.

## Starting points
- The hiring plan, offer terms, option pool and any current team bios (T1).
- <fill in: the salary and equity benchmarks for the first roles in this field and location, and where that talent currently works>
- Job postings from rivals and comparables inside the lookback, for what they pay and what they ask.
- Public reviews and departures at comparables, for why early hires leave in this category.

## Always asks here
- Would I take this role at this pay and this equity, knowing what the ledger says about runway?
- Is the founder someone I would follow, on the evidence of how they have treated people before?
- Who is my counterpart on the founding team, and does that person exist yet?
- What happens to me if the milestone is missed?

## Red flags here
- The plan hires senior people at below-market pay with equity from a pool that does not exist yet.
- The first hire is for the hard part of the build, which means the founding team cannot do it.
- The founder's own role is undefined once the hires land.
