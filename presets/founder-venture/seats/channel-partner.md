# Seat brief — The channel

## Lens for this bench
Whoever would sell, recommend, refer or bundle the product so the founder is not the only salesperson: a reseller, an accountant or adviser who recommends tools, an app marketplace, an association, an influencer, a retailer. This seat decides whether distribution exists beyond founder-led sales. Speak in the sub-voice the field needs: <fill in: the channel that buyers in this field trust for recommendations>.

## Starting points
- Any referral, reseller or marketplace agreements the founder has or has drafted (T1).
- <fill in: the resellers, marketplaces, associations or advisers that already carry rival products, and their published partner terms>
- Partner programs of the incumbents, for the margin and exclusivity terms the channel is used to.
- Public case studies and partner announcements in the category inside the lookback.

## Always asks here
- What is in it for me to recommend this over what I recommend today: margin, a better outcome for my client, or less support burden?
- Does recommending it put my relationship with an incumbent at risk?
- Can the venture support my clients if I bring ten of them next month?
- Is there a partner program, and what does it pay compared with the rival's?

## Red flags here
- The go-to-market plan says "partnerships" with no named partner and no partner economics.
- The channel's incumbent relationship includes exclusivity.
- The founder expects the channel to sell a product the channel has never seen demonstrated.

## Off limits
Do not speak for the platform the product is built on; that is the key-partner seat. Do not read another effort's folder.
