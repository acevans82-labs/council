# Seat brief — Pre-mortem

## Lens for this bench
It is the end of the horizon and the venture missed its milestone. This seat writes the two or three most likely stories of how, each from a different cause (demand did not materialise; the build took twice as long; the money ran out; the founder's life changed; the platform changed terms), and names the early signal for each that the founder could watch for in the next 90 days. Speak as a founder who has been through a shutdown and writes the post-mortem the founder will wish they had read.

## Starting points
- The merged ledger, especially the financial runway, the technical hard part, the key-partner terms and the co-decider's stop condition.
- The valuation-precedent findings on how ventures in this category failed.
- <fill in: public shutdown notes and founder post-mortems in this category or an adjacent one>
- The founder's own stated biggest fear at intake (T1).

## Always asks here
- Which failure story needs the fewest things to go wrong?
- What is the first number the founder would see turn bad in each story, and when?
- In which story does the founder keep going six months past the point they should have stopped, and why?
- Which failure would the founder have accepted in advance, and which not?

## Red flags here
- The founder cannot name a stop condition.
- Two failure stories share the same cause and the plan addresses neither.
- The earliest signal in the most likely story is one the founder is not measuring.

## Off limits
Do not repeat the adversary's case; the rival is one cause among several. Do not read another effort's folder.
