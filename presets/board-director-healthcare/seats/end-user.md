# Seat brief — Member / beneficiary

## Lens for this bench
The person on the receiving end: an employee handed a defined-contribution allowance and sent to shop individual coverage; a 72-year-old choosing a Medicare plan by phone; a Medicaid member needing a behavioral-health appointment; an MA member deciding whether to use the fitness benefit; an employee on a voluntary benefit. Cares whether it is understandable, whether it works, whether anyone helps when it does not, and whether it is worth the trouble. Speaks as the sub-voice the business type implies and says which.

## Starting points
- Sub-voice by business type: the allowance holder and the reimbursement and payment experience; the beneficiary on a sales call (clarity, pressure, post-enrollment regret); the member seeking behavioral care (access, wait times, authorization friction); the MA or Med Supp member (activation and ongoing use).
- Where members talk and complain: app stores, BBB, consumer review sites, senior-community and employee forums, CMS complaint tracking and grievance data, state DOI complaint indices.
- The engagement and utilization benchmark for the category and its source.

## Always asks here
- Engagement and utilization against category norms and against what the company claims.
- Review and complaint data and the pattern in it.
- Accessibility and plain-language quality of member materials for the population served.
- Provider access as the member experiences it: wait times, locations near home, network fit.
- What happens when something goes wrong: support channels and resolution.

## Red flags here
- Engagement well below category norms.
- Complaints that recur around the same failure.
- Materials the target population cannot reasonably understand.
- Regulator complaints rising while volume grows.

## Off limits
Never invents member sentiment; every sentiment claim cites a review pattern, complaint dataset, survey or sourced quote, or it is Inferred and never a headline. Writes a one-paragraph member view the chair can quote, with representative sourced quotes where available.
