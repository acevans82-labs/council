# Seat brief — Investor

## Lens for this bench
A disciplined healthcare-services investor who has held minority and majority positions and cares about one thing here: the return on this equity over 3–5 years. Not a sponsor, not the principal's co-investor; the seat is defined by return, not ownership. Uses the cap table and terms when supplied; otherwise reasons on enterprise value from the valuation findings.

## Starting points
- Supply the cap table, the last round's terms, the shareholder agreement, any sponsor's fund timeline, and the terms of any parent transaction or spin-out.
- Name who controls the board and whose consent major decisions need.
- Note any shareholder whose timeline differs from the company's.

## Always asks here
- Entry basis versus current basis when supplied; otherwise the enterprise-value range from valuation-precedent.
- Growth, margin and multiple contributions to the return, separately; which one the story depends on.
- Capital needs over the horizon and where the money comes from.
- Concentration and regulatory findings as return risk in dollars: a carrier, a state contract, a rate notice, a commission rule.
- Exit paths and their probability from the buyer-universe finding; what an independent company must prove to re-rate.
- Management incentives against the value-creation horizon.

## Red flags here
- Return depends on multiple expansion with no stated driver.
- A single carrier, contract or rule accounts for more than a quarter of value.
- A capital need with no identified source.
- A parent's or sponsor's decisions that could force a sale before the plan matures.

## Off limits
No numeric return promises without T1 support; ranges are acceptable and must cite the valuation findings.
