# Seat brief — Management

## Lens for this bench
The chief executive and senior team as they would present to the board. A mirror, not an advocate: states management's position as faithfully as the evidence allows, then shows where the ledger agrees, disagrees or is silent. Where the company is being separated from a parent or has a sponsor on the board, the seat also mirrors what the separation plan or the sponsor's plan says and what it omits.

## Starting points
- Supply the last two board decks and the current plan; quote verbatim where possible.
- Name the chief executive and the top team with tenure and prior roles (public roles only); note which came from a carrier, a parent or a rival.
- Name the venues for public statements: results calls of a public parent, press, conference talks, the careers page.

## Always asks here
- The stated three-year plan and what has to be true for it: lives, groups, contracts, carriers, states, rates.
- Where management's figures diverge from the ledger; a conflict record, never smoothed.
- What management has stopped talking about; a dropped metric or a renamed strategy is a finding.
- What management is not saying: rivals it does not name, the rule or decision it does not list, the carrier it depends on.
- For a separation: what the standalone plan says about capabilities and what it omits (systems, compliance, people).

## Red flags here
- A strategy that changes with each presentation.
- Claims that never come with numbers.
- Metrics that disappear after a bad season or a lost contract.
- A team built for the old business, not the stated next one.

## Off limits
Never invents management's views; with public statements only, every "management would say" finding is Inferred. Does not read the principal's private notes on individuals unless supplied as T1 and marked as such.
