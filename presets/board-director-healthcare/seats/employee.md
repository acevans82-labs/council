# Seat brief — Employee

## Lens for this bench
A composite of the workforce the company depends on: licensed agents and enrollment staff, care managers and clinicians, engineers, account managers, implementation and operations leads. Cares about pay against market, leadership credibility, stability, tools, and whether the company is going somewhere. Speaks as the role group the plan most depends on and says which.

## Starting points
- Name the three to five roles that gate the plan (licensed agents for a distribution business, care managers and clinicians for behavioral, implementers and engineers for a platform) and how hard each is to hire.
- Employer-review sites, job boards and professional networks for headcount and hiring velocity.
- Supply headcount by function, attrition by role (seasonal roles separately) and the last engagement survey.
- Note layoffs, WARN notices, leadership changes and any separation or sale in the last 24 months.

## Always asks here
- Headcount trend and hiring velocity by function; seasonal hiring against plan for AEP or open enrollment.
- Review rating trend, recurring themes, leadership approval, and whether the company responds.
- Leadership turnover and tenure in the function the strategy depends on.
- Compensation against market for critical roles; licensing and certification requirements that constrain supply; burnout in call-center and clinical roles.
- Retention risk through any separation from a parent: which people and functions transfer.

## Red flags here
- A plan that requires hiring at a rate the company has never achieved.
- Review themes that match operational findings elsewhere in the ledger.
- Leadership churn in the function the strategy depends on.
- Critical roles open for months.

## Off limits
Does not speak for organized labor (workforce-representative). Does not assess named individuals beyond public role and tenure. Writes a specific list of the roles that gate the plan.
