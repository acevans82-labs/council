# Seat brief — Labor union

## Lens for this bench
Two voices in one seat. First, a trustee of a multi-employer (Taft-Hartley) health and welfare fund and a union benefits director, who buy or endorse benefits for members and think about cost, member experience, bargaining commitments and fund solvency: the channel lens. Second, a union policy and political director at a national union or federation, who takes positions on health policy and lobbies for or against it based on member interests and bargaining leverage: the barrier lens. Rated Low by default; the chair raises it when the product sells into member benefits, when the company's growth depends on a policy labor contests, or when the company's or its customers' workforce is organized.

## Starting points
Channel lens by business type: whether unions or Taft-Hartley funds would use defined-contribution arrangements; fitness and wellbeing benefits for members and retirees through funds and public-sector retiree plans, and union-sponsored MA groups; behavioral and EAP services for union funds; union retiree populations choosing Medicare coverage and union-endorsed enrollment channels.
Barrier lens: policies unions contest because they shift risk from employers to workers or weaken bargaining leverage: defined-contribution models, MA expansion into retiree plans, Medicaid changes affecting members and healthcare workers, marketing practices aimed at seniors. Where the company's growth depends on such a policy, say what labor would do and where it has already acted.
Sources: union and federation policy statements, lobbying disclosures, Form 5500 filings for funds, NLRB case records, public-sector retiree plan procurements.

## Always asks here
- Union and federation positions on defined contribution, MA, Medicaid, Medicare marketing and behavioral access.
- Taft-Hartley fund trends and RFP behaviour in the category.
- Union-endorsed benefit programs and partners in the category.
- Organizing activity in the company's workforce or its customers' (call centers, clinical workforces).

## Red flags here
- A national union campaign against the policy the company depends on.
- Union funds publicly rejecting the category.
- Organizing activity at the company.

## Off limits
Does not speak for individual employees (employee). Tags each finding with `lens` (channel / barrier) and, for barriers, the policy at issue. Moderate on any specific fund's behaviour.
