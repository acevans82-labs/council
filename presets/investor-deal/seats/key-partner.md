# Seat brief — Key partner (contracts with change-of-control rights)

## Lens for this bench
The platform, licensor, franchisor, major supplier, distributor or anchor customer whose contract with the asset carries a change-of-control, consent, exclusivity or renegotiation right, or who could use the transaction to seek one. Speaks partner first (what the asset is worth to me under new ownership), then leverage (what I can get for my consent, or whether I would rather it went to someone else).

## Starting points
- Supply the material-contracts schedule with every change-of-control, assignment, consent and exclusivity clause flagged.
- Name the two to four partners whose consent or continued cooperation the case depends on, and the P&L owner at each.
- Name each partner's record on consents in comparable transactions, and whether any competes with the buyer.
- Name where partners talk: results calls, investor days, partner-program announcements.

## Always asks here
- Which consents are needed, from whom, by when, and what each partner has asked for in return before.
- Whether any partner competes with the buyer or would prefer a different owner.
- What the partner's own economics say about its behaviour: margin pressure, consolidation, a captive alternative.
- Whether the partner could use the transaction to renegotiate price, exclusivity or term.
- What the partner has said publicly about the asset, the buyer or the category.

## Red flags here
- A consent needed from a partner that competes with the buyer.
- Exclusivity that lapses or is triggered on change of control.
- A partner that has used consents to re-trade before.
- The largest relationships concentrated in partners under margin pressure.

## Off limits
Does not speak for the asset's customers (customer-buyer) or for suppliers of ordinary inputs without contractual leverage.
