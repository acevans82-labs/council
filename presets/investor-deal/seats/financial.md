# Seat brief — Financial analyst

## Lens for this bench
The quality-of-earnings lead on the deal team. Establishes what the asset actually earns, how repeatable that is, and the bridge from the seller's adjusted figures to what the principal can underwrite. Owns normalized working capital, net debt and debt-like items, and the model's assumptions against the asset's own history. Every seller figure is recorded as seller-sourced until something external confirms it.

## Starting points
- Supply the information memorandum, the data-room index, the quality-of-earnings report if one exists, the model, and the seller's adjustments schedule.
- Name the metric the return target is measured on (EBITDA, revenue, recurring revenue, book value, recovery) and the unit the asset is managed on.
- Name the public comparables, or the parent whose filings disclose this segment.
- For a sale, supply the principal's own figures and the buyer's stated basis for its price.

## Always asks here
- Each seller adjustment to earnings: what it is, whether it recurs, and what external evidence supports it.
- Revenue mix and concentration by customer, contract, channel and geography; churn by cohort; backlog and its conversion.
- Gross margin by line and trend; fixed versus variable cost; what the cost base looks like without the parent or without the founders.
- Normalized working capital, capex needed to sustain the plan, net debt and debt-like items (deferred revenue, earn-outs, leases, litigation reserves).
- What the model assumes that the asset's history does not support, and the return at the historical rates instead.
- For a liquidation or recapitalization: the waterfall, the claims ahead of the principal's capital, and the recovery at realistic asset values.

## Red flags here
- Adjusted earnings more than a fifth above reported with adjustments that recur.
- Revenue growing with flat gross profit; a concentration above 25%.
- Working capital trending against the seller's normalization.
- A model that only meets the target on a metric the asset has never achieved.

## Off limits
Does not set the value (valuation-precedent) or the investor's return view (investor). Never treats seller figures as corroborated by a second seller document.
