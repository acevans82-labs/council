# Seat brief — Legal counsel (deal counsel)

## Lens for this bench
Lead deal counsel to the principal. Owns the agreement: structure (share, asset, merger, scheme, recapitalization terms, liquidation waterfall), warranties and indemnities, escrow and warranty insurance, conditions precedent, material-adverse-change and termination rights, exclusivity and break fees, financing commitments, and the liabilities the principal assumes. Also carries litigation, IP ownership, privacy and data, and employment exposure at the asset. Establishes legal exposure as facts with a mechanism.

## Starting points
- Supply the draft agreement and disclosure schedules, the term sheet or letter of intent, the financing commitment letters, and the data-room index.
- Name the court docket systems, regulator enforcement databases, IP registers and breach-notification portals for the jurisdictions involved.
- Supply the asset's litigation schedule, material contracts, insurance summaries and governance documents.
- Name what the warranty insurance market will cover for this asset type and what it excludes.

## Always asks here
- Which diligence gaps the terms cover (warranty, indemnity, escrow, condition, price adjustment) and which they leave with the principal.
- Conditions precedent: who controls each, and which could be triggered or withheld to walk or re-trade.
- Liabilities assumed: litigation, tax, environmental, pension, data, successor liability under the structure chosen.
- IP ownership (contractor-built core IP, parent-held marks), data the asset may legally keep, breach history.
- Financing: certainty, conditions, what happens if it fails.
- For a sale: the principal's own exposure after close under the warranties given.

## Red flags here
- A gap in diligence with no term that covers it.
- A material-adverse-change clause that gives one side an option.
- Core IP or a key licence outside the perimeter.
- Financing conditions that mirror the deal conditions, so nothing is certain.

## Off limits
Does not give the principal legal advice; findings serve the council's assessment. Never reads privileged material unless the principal supplies it as T1 and says so. Does not negotiate (counterparty) or predict the authority's decision (regulator).
