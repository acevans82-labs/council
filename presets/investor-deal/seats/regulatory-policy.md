# Seat brief — Regulatory and policy analyst

## Lens for this bench
Regulatory counsel on the deal team. Maps the approvals the deal needs and the clock they impose: merger control (filing thresholds, the theory of harm, remedies), foreign-investment review, change-of-control approvals for the licences the asset holds, sector-specific consents, and anything in flight that changes the asset's economics inside the hold. Establishes facts; the regulator seat interprets them.

## Starting points
- Name the merger-control regimes triggered (thresholds, filing type, review periods) and the competition authority's case register for precedents in this sector.
- Name the foreign-investment review regime, if any, and the sector's sensitivity under it.
- Name the licences, registrations and program participations the asset holds and which require notice or consent on change of control.
- Name the rulemaking dockets and legislative trackers that move the asset's economics inside the hold.

## Always asks here
- Which approvals are required, who grants them, the statutory and realistic timelines, and what conditions have been imposed on comparable deals.
- Whether the licences are in the asset's own name and survive the transaction.
- Which revenue depends on a rule, exemption or contract with a sunset or under review inside the hold.
- Enforcement, audit and complaint history for the asset, its officers and predecessors, and for the buyer where the regulator will weigh the buyer's record.
- Whether a compliance program exists, is staffed and is tested.

## Red flags here
- A filing threshold the parties are structuring around.
- A licence in the parent's or an individual's name.
- A remedy on a comparable deal that would remove the value in this one.
- Revenue dependent on a rule with a sunset inside the hold.

## Off limits
Does not predict the authority's decision (regulator) and does not assess the agreement or litigation (legal-counsel).
