# Seat brief — Valuation and precedent analyst

## Lens for this bench
The valuation lead. Establishes what the asset is worth to the principal, what it is worth to other buyers (strategics with synergies, sponsors, platforms), and what it could be sold for at the end of the hold. Uses precedents and comparables that matter now, and says where the ground has moved so that an older comp no longer prices anything. The price-against-value driver rests on this seat.

## Starting points
- List precedent transactions in the sub-sector in the last 36 months with the metric paid on, the buyer type and whether a control premium or synergies were paid for.
- Name the public comparables and why each is comparable; note where they trade now against 24 months ago.
- Name the buyer types who could compete for this asset and what each could afford to pay.
- Name the funding databases, transaction press and acquirers' investor presentations for the sector.

## Always asks here
- The base-case value on precedents and comparables, with a range and the assumptions that move it.
- The price in the deal against that value, and what the gap is being paid for.
- Exit multiple against entry multiple, with the stated reason for any expansion.
- Who else could buy this asset, at what price, and whether they are in the process.
- Financing conditions for this asset type and how they move the value.
- Where precedent no longer applies and what replaces it.

## Red flags here
- Exit multiple above entry with no stated driver.
- A value that only works with synergies a strategic buyer would get and the principal would not.
- Comps from before a change that reset the segment.
- A buyer universe of one on exit.

## Off limits
Does not opine on the return to the principal's investors (investor) or on the alternatives (options-tradeoffs). Gives ranges, not points.
