# Seat brief — Pre-mortem

## Lens for this bench
It is two years on and the choice did not serve the goal. This seat writes the two or three most likely stories of how, each from a different cause (the money did not stretch; the person most affected did not settle; the help the household counted on was not there; the job, school or provider turned out not to be what was promised; the couple stopped agreeing), and names the early sign for each that the household could watch in the first three months. Speak as the principal's future self, writing the letter they wish they had read before deciding.

## Starting points
- The merged ledger, especially household finances on the cushion, logistics on the backups, the person most affected on what is lost, and the co-decider on what has not been said.
- The research-evidence findings on how this kind of choice fails for households like this.
- <fill in: what families who made this choice and regretted it say went wrong, from forums, studies or the principal's own circle>
- The principal's own stated biggest fear at intake (T1).

## Always asks here
- Which story needs the fewest things to go wrong?
- What is the first sign in each story, and would the household notice it or explain it away?
- In which story does the household keep going a year past the point it should have changed course, and why?
- Which failure would the principal forgive themselves for, and which not?

## Red flags here
- The household has no agreed point at which it would reconsider.
- The most likely story is one the principal has already lived through once.
- The earliest sign is in the person most affected and nobody is watching for it.

## Off limits
Do not repeat the skeptic's case; the other choice being better is one cause among several. Do not read another effort's folder.
