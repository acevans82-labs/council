# Primary and regulatory sources by kind of effort

A starter list. A bench's `bench.md` names the sources that matter in its field under "Primary sources in this field"; seat briefs add the specific registers. Extend this file by pull request when a field is missing. US-centric where it names agencies; the pattern (register, regulator, court, statistics office, journal index) holds everywhere.

## Any organization
- Corporate registers: securities filings (SEC EDGAR and its equivalents), company registries, charity and non-profit filings (Form 990)
- Court dockets and enforcement actions: federal and state courts, attorneys general, consumer-protection and competition authorities
- Trademark and patent registers
- Data-protection and breach registers; sanctions and exclusion lists for owners and officers
- Procurement and contract-award portals for public-sector customers
- Official statistics offices for market sizing denominators

## Regulated products and services (finance, insurance, health, energy, telecoms, transport)
- The sector regulator's rulebook, bulletins, enforcement database and complaint statistics
- License, registration and appointment lookups (producers, administrators, advisers, operators)
- Rate, product and tariff filings where public
- Standards bodies and accreditation registers
- The rulemaking record: federal register equivalents, consultation papers, legislative bill trackers, committee hearings

## Healthcare and benefits (US)
- CMS rules, memos, rate notices, enrollment and complaint data; CCIIO marketplace rules; Medicaid managed-care rules and state contract awards
- State insurance departments: licensing, SERFF filings, market-conduct exams, complaint indices; NAIC statements
- Treasury, DOL and HHS guidance on employer benefits and tax-advantaged arrangements; EBSA enforcement; Form 5500
- Provider registers (NPPES, PECOS), cost reports, network-adequacy filings, parity enforcement reports
- OIG and GAO reports; OCR breach portal

## Technology, data and AI
- Standards and certifications (SOC 2, ISO 27001, FedRAMP), attestation letters, status pages, incident disclosures
- Open-source dependency and vulnerability databases; model cards and evaluation reports
- Regulators' AI and data guidance (FTC, EU AI Act bodies, data-protection authorities)
- Patent filings and research preprints for capability claims

## Employment and workforce
- Labor-relations boards (organizing petitions), layoff notices (WARN), wage and hour enforcement
- Professional licensing boards and association positions; union and federation statements; lobbying disclosures
- Review sites and job postings as capability and morale signals (T3; corroborate)

## Education, family and personal decisions
- Education departments and school report cards; accreditation registers; inspection reports
- Public health agencies and clinical guideline bodies; systematic-review databases (Cochrane, PubMed, trial registries)
- Consumer-protection and product-safety agencies; local government records (zoning, permits, inspections)
- Benefits and eligibility rules for public programs that touch the decision

## Research questions
- Peer-reviewed journals and preprint servers; systematic reviews and meta-analyses; trial and study registries
- Official datasets and their documentation; replication reports; retraction databases
- Professional-society position statements and the dissenting literature they cite
